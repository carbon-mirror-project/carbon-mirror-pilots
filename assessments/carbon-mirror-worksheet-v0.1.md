# Method B — Carbon Mirror Four Mirrors assessment (draft)

Reviewer: __________  Date: __________  Baseline revision: __________  Time budget: __________

For every Mirror: record **applicability, evidence, observed issue, proposed intervention, measurable prediction, business constraint, possible negative effect, falsification test**. Not-applicable is a valid finding.

## Efficiency
- What work is repeated or provisioned beyond operational need?
- Are CPU/memory allocations or algorithms appropriate to the task?

## Timing
- Are there deadlines and valid deferral windows?
- Is there an actual time-varying tariff or emissions input? If no, mark the benefit hypothetical.

## Data Diet
- Is anything read, written, moved, duplicated or retained without a defined purpose?
- Does an intermediate artifact provide required auditability or recovery value?

## Circularity
- Can legitimate intermediate work be reused instead of recalculated?
- Is any physical heat or materials recovery actually relevant? If not, do not invent a score.

## Cross-Mirror consequences
- Does a change that saves storage increase CPU or memory?
- Does compression worsen latency, retrieval or auditability?
- Who benefits and who carries operational risk?

For each observation: **ID | Mirror | evidence | hypothesis | expected gain | possible loss | validation test | priority**.

This worksheet is a diagnostic, **not** the standard's composite scoring or certification mechanism.
