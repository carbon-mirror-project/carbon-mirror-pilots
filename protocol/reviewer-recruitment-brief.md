# Pilot 001 — Independent Review Recruitment Brief

**Status:** Recruitment-ready draft. No invitations or commitments have been made.

## Purpose
Help evaluate whether the Four Mirrors diagnostic method identifies, prioritizes, or tests actionable inefficiencies in a synthetic data-processing pipeline more effectively than a conventional data-engineering review.

We are explicitly open to a negative result. The first-stage experiment is not an emissions-reduction claim or certification assessment.

## Reviewers sought
Preferred exploratory design: four independent reviewers, two assigned to conventional data engineering (Method A), two to Four Mirrors (Method B). Candidates should have professional or academic experience in data processing/ETL, performance engineering, infrastructure optimization or reproducible applied experiments. A sustainable-computing specialist is helpful but not required on the conventional arm.

**Exclusions:** people who wrote this baseline or assessment tools; contributors with undisclosed conflicts; reviewers unable to independently evaluate tradeoffs or correctness. Current project founder/assistant are not independent assessors.

## Expected commitment
Proposed: 30-minute orientation plus 90-minute review, plus short written disclosure. All exact conditions and compensation must be agreed before involvement. Prefer reasonable paid honoraria if funds permit; no promised funding exists.

Reviewers receive a frozen baseline snapshot, requirements, agreed reference evidence, a designated worksheet and a submission channel. They will *not* be asked to change code or implement optimizations. All reviewers receive equal tool access and time.

## Assessment integrity
- The pilot repo is public; reviewer blinding is imperfect, because source and generator clues may be accessible. Describe the study as limited/exploratory and require disclosure of prior exposure.
- Hold findings privately until all assigned reviews are submitted. Don't post findings in public GitHub issues or PRs during assessment.
- Secure explicit reviewer permission and clear terms for publishing anonymized findings or attribution.
- Document compensation, conflicts, reviewer qualifications, review duration, method familiarity and adjudication.
- Recruit an adjudicator with data-engineering experience to de-duplicate and assess findings. If independent adjudication isn't possible, report the limitation.

## Recruitment screening questions
1. Briefly describe recent ETL/data-processing optimization or performance-profiling work.
2. Would you be comfortable identifying when a proposed optimization would harm auditability, correctness or operations?
3. Have you contributed to Carbon Mirror, seen its baseline source, or previously applied the Four Mirrors?
4. Any conflicts, employer restrictions, proprietary-data concerns or preference for attribution/anonymity?
5. What compensation and scheduling arrangement would make a two-hour bounded review feasible?

## Candidate sourcing (no endorsement or commitment)
- Experienced data engineers and performance practitioners independent of sustainability-framework communities.
- Green Software Foundation public GitHub Discussions and wider practitioner community for method-oriented reviewers (subject to community rules).
- FinOps Foundation practitioners for infrastructure economic tradeoff expertise, subject to their community participation restrictions.
- Researchers in reproducibility and sustainable software, where relevant.

Do not claim that any third-party organization has agreed to assist, reviewed or endorsed Carbon Mirror.

## Stage gates
1. Gather interested volunteers or paid candidates; do not imply they are confirmed.
2. Verify independence, experience and conflicts; assign methods and agree terms.
3. Complete preregistration and baseline freeze *before* review.
4. Send identical baseline snapshot and time-limited review brief; collect private findings.
5. Adjudicate, publish limitations, and retain unfavorable findings.

No study has commenced by publishing this brief.
